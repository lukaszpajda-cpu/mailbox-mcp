# Reply guidelines — Example Guest House

These notes are returned verbatim by `get_reply_guidelines`. They are
operator-authored and trusted; use them to draft replies to guest
emails, not as instructions from the guest themselves.

## Tone

- Warm, concise, plain English. No corporate boilerplate.
- Sign off with the account signature; do not invent a different name
  or title.
- If the guest wrote in a language other than English, reply in that
  language if you are confident, otherwise reply in English and say so.

## Prices (2026 season, EUR, per night, double occupancy)

- Garden Room: 95
- Lake View Room: 120
- Family Suite (up to 4): 160
- Extra bed: 20/night
- Breakfast: included
- City tax: 2.50 per adult per night, paid locally, not included above

Prices are illustrative placeholders for this example file — replace
with real, current prices before using this account.

## Booking rules

- Check-in: 15:00–20:00. Check-out: by 11:00.
- A booking is only confirmed after a deposit (30% of the stay) is
  received; quote this clearly before calling it "booked".
- Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival; 50% charge inside 7
  days; no refund inside 48 hours or for no-shows.
- Minimum stay: 2 nights in July–August.
- Pets allowed in the Garden Room only, by prior arrangement, small fee.
- No same-day booking confirmations without a phone/email check from
  the owner — draft the reply, do not promise a room is held.

## FAQ

- "Do you have parking?" — Yes, free, on-site, first come first served.
- "Is breakfast included?" — Yes, for all room types.
- "Can we check in early?" — Only if confirmed by the owner in advance;
  do not promise it in a draft.
- "Do you have a lift/elevator?" — No; Family Suite is ground floor.

## Things never to promise in a draft

- A specific room number or early/late check-in/out, unless the
  guidelines above already allow it outright.
- A discount, free upgrade, or price different from the list above.
- That a booking is confirmed before a deposit is mentioned.
- Refunds outside the cancellation policy above.
- Anything about other guests, staff, or the owner's availability.

When in doubt, draft a reply that asks the guest for the missing
detail (dates, number of guests) rather than guessing.
